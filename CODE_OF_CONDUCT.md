# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing impact, respect privacy and boundaries, and make technical disagreement about the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@opensoftware.co` with the subject “Community conduct.” Do not open a public issue about an individual. Include only the context needed to investigate and do not send unrelated personal or customer data.

Maintainers will keep reports as confidential as practical, recuse anyone with a conflict of interest, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include a private correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.

This policy applies to repository activity, project communication channels, events, and public representation of the project.
