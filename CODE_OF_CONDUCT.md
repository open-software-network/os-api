# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Be considerate, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and accept
constructive feedback. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and disclosure of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@opensoftware.co`. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with fair consideration for
everyone involved.
